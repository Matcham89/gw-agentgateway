# Poppy Assistant

Poppy Assistant is a **personal, single-user AI assistant** that helps its owner
manage their own Gmail and Google Calendar. It is not a public service, and no one
other than its owner can sign in to it or use it.

## What it does

- **Gmail:** searches and reads the owner's email, and applies or removes labels.
  It cannot send, draft, delete, or trash email.
- **Google Calendar:** lists and searches events, and creates, updates, or responds
  to events on the owner's request. It cannot delete events.

Typical requests: "What emails did I get from my accountant this week?" or
"What is on my calendar tomorrow?"

## Who runs it

The app is built and operated by Chris Matcham for personal use, on a private
server. It runs through a small gateway that holds the Google credentials and
limits which actions the assistant is allowed to take.

## Google user data

Poppy Assistant requests these Google scopes: Gmail read-only, Gmail labels,
Calendar events, Calendar events read-only, Calendar list read-only, and Calendar
free/busy. See the [privacy policy](privacy) for exactly how that data is used,
stored, and protected.

Poppy Assistant's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.
